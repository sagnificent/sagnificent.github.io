About me

I'm Sagnik Roy, a third-year Bachelor of Statistics student at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata. My interests lie in Single-Cell and Spatial Transcriptomics, Statistical Machine Learning, and Bayesian Data Analysis.

Most of my work so far has been applied: modelling lagged relationships between tiger mortality and population growth in India, analysing single-cell RNA-seq data to study myeloid–fibroblast interactions in lung fibrosis, and building predictive models for earthquake intensity and stock movement from text. I like problems where a careful statistical argument does more work than a bigger model.
